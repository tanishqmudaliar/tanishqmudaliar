## About

Software engineer with an Electronics & Telecommunication foundation, specializing in resource-constrained systems, offline-first client architectures, on-device ML inference & developer tooling.

My engineering approach prioritizes low-latency execution and architectural resilience - solving practical constraints by running neural networks locally on edge hardware, designing offline-first client storage engines & automating cloud maintenance routines.

- **Focus Areas**: Edge AI & local inference (ONNX, MobileBERT, Ollama), offline-first logistics engines & developer tooling.
- **Academic Foundation**: B.E. in Computer Engineering, SIES GST • Diploma in Electronics & Telecommunication, Vidyalankar Polytechnic.
- **Honors**: Smart India Hackathon '25 Grand Finalist • CiiA-3 National Top 100 Innovator.
