## About

I like building things that hold up on their own. A [logistics PWA](https://github.com/tanishqmudaliar/Trippr) that runs invoicing and analytics entirely in the browser with no backend. A [CI bot](https://github.com/tanishqmudaliar/PythonAnywhere-Auto-Renew) that keeps cloud deployments alive so I never have to touch them. A [VS Code extension](https://github.com/tanishqmudaliar/AdbZen) that handles ADB device management from the sidebar. If it can work without babysitting, that's usually where I end up.

I'm a Computer Engineering student with a background in Electronics & Telecommunication and I'm currently getting deeper into ML. I've gone from training models to deploying them on devices and I want to keep pushing in that direction. Right now I'm most interested in the engineering side of ML and figuring out how to take models from experiments to real products.

- **Focus Areas**: Fullstack development (Next.js, Flutter, FastAPI), automation and developer tooling, applied ML.
- **Academic Foundation**: B.E. in Computer Engineering, SIES GST • Diploma in Electronics & Telecommunication, Vidyalankar Polytechnic.
- **Honors**: Smart India Hackathon '25 Grand Finalist • CiiA-3 National Top 100 Innovator.
